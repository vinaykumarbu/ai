# Why a Stack Beats a Single App

Most beginners hunt for one perfect AI app that does everything. That search never ends, because a useful AI worker is not one app. It is a small system with many parts, and each part has one clear job.

Think of your agent like a vehicle made of several mechanisms that work together:

The interface is your steering wheel. It is where you control the agent. In your case, that will be Telegram on your phone and computer.

The model is the engine. This is what actually drives your agent. It is the intelligence that reads, reasons, and writes. In this course, Hermes will use GPT 5.6 Sol as that model.

The brain is your GPS and black book. It is the context and memory layer that makes the agent yours. It knows your business, your goals, and how you communicate. The more context it has, the more accurately it gets you to your destination. You will build this in Obsidian in Module 3.

Tools are your wheels. There is little point to an engine unless it powers something that touches the ground and moves the car. Your tools will include email, Drive, Slack, Discord, Calendar, and Wispr. You will connect these in Module 5.

Boundaries are the brakes and the rules of the road. Speed is useless without the ability to stop. Without rules, your agent can go haywire. The last thing you want is an agent sending emails without your approval. You will set this up in Module 2 and harden it in Module 5.

Hold these five parts in your head: interface, model, brain, tools, boundaries. Your agent is the sum of its parts. A great engine without a drivetrain and wheels will only make beautiful sound.
